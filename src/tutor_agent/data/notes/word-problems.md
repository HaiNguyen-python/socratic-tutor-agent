# Word problems

## Read, name, write

Read the problem twice. Name the unknown with a letter, for example t for tickets or w for
weeks. Write one equation that says the same thing as the story, then solve it.

## Fixed costs and rates

Many problems have a fixed part and a part that grows: "a 10 euro fee plus 3 euros per hour"
is 10 + 3h. The total bill goes on the other side of the equals sign.

## Perimeter and shapes

The perimeter of a rectangle is 2 × (length + width). If the length is described using the
width ("3 cm longer than the width"), write the length as w + 3.

## Speed, distance and time

Distance = speed × time, so time = distance ÷ speed. Keep the units consistent: km and km/h
give hours. A time like 1.5 hours is one and a half hours, or 90 minutes.
